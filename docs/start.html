Spoza UE: najpierw PESEL (wyżej), potem profil zaufany w punkcie potwierdzającym — najlepiej przy tej samej wizycie w gminie. Without a PESEL, an EU citizen can log in with their national e-ID via the European login (eIDAS) and give a European identifier — if their country has such an e-ID. Even so, we recommend getting a PESEL: without it, a Polish business account, eZUS and KSeF are hard. Non-EU: PESEL first (above), then the Trusted Profile at a confirmation point — ideally in the same municipality visit.       • E-mail and phone (can be non-public) and a business account (can be added later).
